# GrayAlgo

**Transparent AI for Healthcare and Finance**

GrayAlgo is an AI-first initiative focused on building ethical, explainable, and bias-free algorithms for high-stakes domains.  
Our mission is to make AI decisions **transparent, neutral, and human-centered** — ensuring trust in healthcare and finance.

---

## 🚀 Vision
- **Healthcare**: Support doctors with explainable diagnostics, bias-free recommendations, and ethical guardrails.
- **Finance**: Deliver transparent fraud detection, fair credit scoring, and compliance-aware insights.
- **Cross-domain**: Build AI that explains *why* it made a decision, not just *what* it decided.

---

## 🛡️ Ethics & Transparency
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Every output comes with reasoning.
- **Bias Auditing**: Continuous checks to remove demographic or systemic bias.
- **Neutrality**: Present multiple perspectives before suggesting an action.
- **Human-in-the-loop**: AI as a co-pilot, never a dictator.

---

## 📂 Planned Repository Structure
- `src/healthcare/` → Diagnostic models, patient data preprocessing
- `src/finance/` → Fraud detection, portfolio risk analysis
- `src/common/` → Shared utilities (data pipelines, explainability modules)
- `docs/` → Vision notes, ethical guidelines, research drafts
- `examples/` → Demo workflows for healthcare and finance
- `tests/` → Bias checks, transparency validation
